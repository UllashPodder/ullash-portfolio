Getting started

1

Open the app and tap the record button. Debrief starts recording your audio.

2

Tap stop when you're done. Your recording is saved locally on your device.

3

Open a recording to see its transcript. Transcription runs on your device.

4

Tap Summarize and choose a format — Meeting Minutes, Brief, Bullet Points, or Action Items.

5

Copy or export your notes to the clipboard or as Markdown.

Frequently asked questions

Does Debrief work offline?▾

Yes. Once the app and its AI model are installed, transcription and summarization run entirely on your device with no internet connection required.

Do my recordings go to the cloud?▾

No. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers that receive your recordings, transcripts, or summaries.

Why does the app download a file on first launch?▾

To run AI summarization on your device, the app downloads an AI model once. This is a one-time download (around 1–2 GB). We recommend doing it over Wi-Fi. After that, summarization works offline.

Which devices are supported?▾

Debrief Notes supports iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Some features may perform better on newer devices with more memory.

The transcript has mistakes. Can I fix them?▾

Where supported, you can edit a transcript before generating a summary.

How do I delete a recording?▾

Delete it within the app. To remove everything, delete the app — all recordings and notes are stored locally and are removed with it.

Is there an account or subscription?▾

No account is required, and there are no per-meeting or token fees for the core features.
